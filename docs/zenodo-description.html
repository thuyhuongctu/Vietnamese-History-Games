Vietnamese History Games (Cổng Trò Chơi Sử Việt, ThuyHuong Digital 2026) is a portal of five browser-based educational games set in documented periods of Vietnamese history. The games are written in HTML5 and JavaScript, require no installation or user account, and are published as a Progressive Web Application: once loaded, the portal installs to a device home screen, runs full screen, and remains playable without a network connection.

Contents of the deposit. The archive contains the complete, independently executable source of the portal and of each game, together with the application manifest, service worker, icon set, images, audio files, privacy policy, citation metadata and release documentation. No build step, server-side component or content delivery network is required: the repository is the deployable application.

The games.

  • Vân Đồn: The First Trading Port (1149). A construction and port management game beginning with the establishment of Vân Đồn in the reign of King Lý Anh Tông, commonly regarded as the first international trading port of Đại Việt. The player builds piers for merchant ships from Song China, Srivijaya, Java and Siam, opens pottery kilns, silk workshops and pearl-diving stations, collects tax revenue, weathers storms and repels raids. The game includes tiered building upgrades, royal tribute quests, an in-game history codex, an achievements board, and a challenge mode recreating the Battle of Vân Đồn (1288), in which Trần Khánh Dư intercepted the Yuan supply fleet.
  • Vân Đồn 3D: Walking the Quay (1149–1348). The player walks the quay in three dimensions as a court official or a woman merchant, across six chapters. Three chapters follow entries in the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư (the founding in 1149, the arrival of Siamese and Srivijayan merchants in 1184, and the Đồ Bồ ship secretly buying pearls in 1348); the other three are constructed by the game. Ships arrive one by one with orders and short waiting times, prices change between chapters, and the player can build carts, a larger kiln, a naval watchtower and a sea shrine. Characters are clay figures; the end screen closes with reflection questions on trade-offs for class discussion.
  • Bắc Hải Đảo: Pirates of the Gulf of Tonkin (1780). A maritime adventure among the limestone karsts of Hạ Long Bay. The player sails a bat-wing junk between six ports, trades fish sauce, silk, agarwood and pearls, engages Tàu Ô pirate vessels, survives storms and finally confronts the pirate lord Hắc Giao. Four playable captains provide distinct starting conditions and abilities. Characters and story are fiction.
  • Bắc Hải Đảo 3D (1780). The same world rebuilt in three dimensions with three.js: a chase camera, animated sea surface, limestone islands, three-dimensional ports and vessels, and storm weather. Both editions are retained so that the portal continues to run on low-powered devices.
  • Chợ Nổi: Floating Market (nineteenth century). An arcade game set in the floating markets that formed along the canals of the Mekong Delta during the settlement of the southern provinces. Built on the Phaser 3 engine.

History and fiction. The portal labels the Vân Đồn games as based on historical records and the Bắc Hải Đảo games as fiction. Dates, nations and events cited in the Vân Đồn games come from the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư, and each game lists separately what the record states and what the game simplifies or invents. All five games are nevertheless works of interactive fiction: their mechanics, difficulty balance and narrative detail are designed for playability rather than as a scholarly source.

Sound and music. In-game background music is synthesised in real time with the Web Audio API on the Vietnamese pentatonic scale, emulating đàn tranh plucked strings and đàn bầu pitch bends. The deposit also contains four songs sung in Vietnamese (MP3), each with its lyrics in the game or on the portal: the theme songs of Vân Đồn 3D and Bắc Hải Đảo, a song shared by both settings, and the portal's own song, which also has a music video (MP4). Songs are downloaded only when a player first listens and are then cached for offline use.

Technical characteristics. Input is by keyboard on desktop and by touch on mobile devices. The two external libraries used, three.js (r147) and Phaser (3.90.0), are vendored into the repository under their own MIT licences so that each archived release is complete and independently executable.

Intended use. The portal is intended for upper-secondary and undergraduate teaching, for self-directed learning, and for general audiences aged 16 and over who are interested in Vietnamese history. Because it runs on low-specification hardware and continues to work offline after the first load, it is usable in classrooms without reliable internet access.

Privacy. The application collects no personal data. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party runtime requests and no server-side component. Saved games, high scores and preferences such as sound and language are written to browser storage on the user's own device.

Language. The interface of the portal and of every game is available in English and Vietnamese; the application opens in English and remembers the player's choice on the device. Song lyrics are in Vietnamese. Documentation is provided in English and Vietnamese.

Availability. Playable at games.jemappellehuong.com; source repository at github.com/thuyhuongctu/Vietnamese-History-Games. The games are free to play and to teach with. All rights are reserved by the author; redistribution, modification and commercial use require prior written permission.

Tiếng Việt: Cổng Trò Chơi Sử Việt gồm năm trò chơi giáo dục lịch sử Việt Nam chạy trực tiếp trên trình duyệt: Vân Đồn (1149), Vân Đồn 3D (1149–1348), Bắc Hải Đảo (1780) bản 2D và bản 3D, và Chợ Nổi (thế kỷ XIX). Giao diện có tiếng Anh và tiếng Việt, kèm bốn bài hát tiếng Việt và một video ca nhạc. Cổng game cài được lên màn hình chính như một ứng dụng, chơi được khi không có mạng, không quảng cáo và không thu thập bất kỳ dữ liệu cá nhân nào. Dành cho dạy học, tự học và người từ 16 tuổi quan tâm tới lịch sử Việt Nam.
